# Icon DNA — Health & Wellness

Fitness, meditation, sleep, nutrition, medical, mental health. The category where the icon must
not induce the anxiety the product is meant to relieve.

## 1. Mental model

Health apps are opened in a vulnerable state — before a workout, during insomnia, after a
difficult day. The icon sits on the home screen as a **standing reminder** of an intention the
user may or may not be keeping.

This makes the emotional register unusually consequential. An aggressive fitness icon on the
home screen of someone who has not exercised in three weeks is a small daily reproach, and
people delete apps to stop feeling that.

| Sub-register | Register |
|---|---|
| Performance / fitness | Energetic but not aggressive; deep colour, precise form |
| Calm / meditation / sleep | Very low saturation, soft edges, spacious, quiet |
| Medical / clinical | Precise, trustworthy, restrained — but not cold |
| Nutrition | Warm, natural, organic, appetising |

The calm sub-register has the strictest constraints in the entire skill: an icon for a sleep
or meditation app that is even slightly loud has failed at its function.

## 2. Metaphors

**Exhausted:**
- Heart (both the anatomical and the symbol)
- Pulse / ECG line
- Water drop
- Lotus flower / meditation pose figure
- Dumbbell
- Leaf (nutrition)
- Cross / caduceus (medical)

**In use:** Apple Health = white heart on pink · Apple Fitness = activity rings ·
Calm = blue gradient wordmark · Headspace = orange circle-face · Strava = orange chevron ·
Whoop = red-black minimal · Oura = black ring · Flo = pink

**Open:**
- **Breathing form** — a shape that visibly implies expansion and contraction; the single best
  meditation metaphor available and rarely used well
- **Still water / settled surface** — calm; excellent material affordance
- **Balanced stone stack** — equilibrium, patience
- **Sun arc / horizon** — daily rhythm, sleep-wake cycle
- **Vessel filling** — restoration, hydration, recovery without a water drop
- **Coil / spring** — stored energy, recovery, potential
- **Two forms in resonance** — mind-body alignment
- **Growth ring / annulus** — accumulated consistency

## 3. Shape language

- **Circle** — the dominant and correct shape here: calm, complete, balanced, non-directional
- **Ring** — cycles, closing a loop, consistency (Apple owns activity rings specifically —
  three concentric coloured rings is off-limits)
- **Organic blob** — softness, breath, the body
- **Arc** — rhythm, the day's curve
- **Avoid:** triangles and sharp angles in the calm sub-register; they inject urgency

## 4. Material

| Role | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Primary | **Glazed ceramic** — warm, calm, tactile, human |
| Accent | Frosted glass, or a soft luminous core |
| Reflection | Subtle |
| Transparency | 10–25% |
| Edge | Soft rounded — no hard edges anywhere in the calm register |
| Finish | Matte to satin. **Not gloss** — gloss reads as clinical or as plastic. |

Ceramic is close to the ideal material for this category: it is warm, it is calm, it has
genuine subsurface behaviour, and it is nearly unused here.

## 5. Palette

**Calm:**
```
Soft teal #14B8A6 · Warm off-white #FEFCE8 · Sand #E7E5E4
```
Keep saturation under 60%. This is the one category where under-saturating is almost always
right.

**Performance:**
```
Deep indigo #312E81 · Coral #FB7185 · Bone #F5F5F4
```
Energy through depth of colour, not through saturation or sharp angles.

**Nutrition:**
```
Sage #84A98C · Terracotta #C2673F · Cream #FEF3C7
```

**Medical:**
```
Muted teal #0F766E · Bone #F5F5F4 · Slate #334155
```
Avoid clinical white-and-blue, which reads as insurance software.

**Collision map:** white heart on pink → Apple Health · three concentric rings → Apple Fitness
· blue gradient → Calm · orange circle → Headspace · orange chevron → Strava · black ring →
Oura.

## 6. Traps

| Trap | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| A heart | The most exhausted glyph in the category, and Apple Health owns it at system level |
| Pulse / ECG line | Thin stroke, dies at 40px; also universal |
| A lotus | Meditation cliché; also culturally loaded in ways that vary by market |
| Three concentric coloured rings | Apple Fitness owns this specifically |
| Aggression in a calm product | The icon becomes a daily reproach |
| High saturation in sleep/meditation | Contradicts the product's core promise |
| Clinical blue-and-white | Reads as insurance, not wellbeing |
| A human figure | Excludes; also unreadable at 40px |

## 7. Prompt fragments

**Calm ceramic:**
```
a single smooth glazed ceramic form in warm off-white, extremely simple silhouette,
soft subsurface scattering, very gentle broad specular from a large diffuse light above,
matte satin glaze, soft rounded edges, muted sand field, quiet and still
```

**Breathing form:**
```
a soft rounded form caught mid-expansion, gentle asymmetry implying motion without direction,
frosted-glass translucency with a soft luminous core,
diffuse light scatter, no sharp specular, very low saturation teal and cream
```

**Still water:**
```
a perfectly still liquid surface held in a shallow ceramic vessel,
one single soft reflection of the light above, no ripples,
matte ceramic rim, muted teal water, warm off-white field, deeply calm
```

**Category negatives:**
```
heart, anatomical heart, pulse line, ECG, heartbeat, water drop, lotus, flower, meditation pose,
yoga figure, human figure, dumbbell, barbell, running figure, leaf, apple, medical cross,
caduceus, stethoscope, pill, syringe, concentric rings, clinical, high saturation, glossy
```
